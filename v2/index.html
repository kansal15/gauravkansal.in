About Me

I am Gaurav Kansal. I am a Joint Director (IT) at the National Informatics Centre (NIC), Government of India. I have been with NIC since 2010.

At NIC, I have worked on many important national projects. These include the National Knowledge Network (NKN), IPv6 rollout across NICNET, Anycast DNS services, and Wi-Fi services for Government offices across the country. I also built TLS Labs, a reference on TLS, certificates and cryptography, used inside NIC.

Today, I am the Technical Lead for Bharat Public DNS (1.10.10.10), also known as Sarvagya. It is India's own public DNS service. It answers more than 5 billion queries every day and helps make India's internet safer. In 2026, the project won the Technology Sabha Enterprise Security award.

I am active in the global internet community. I was Co-Chair of the APNIC NIR SIG from 2023 to 2024. I was a member of the NRO Number Council from 2023 to 2024. I am a member of the RSSAC Caucus. Since 2023, I have represented RSSAC on the ICANN Fellowship Selection Committee, which selects the fellows for ICANN meetings. I also work closely with IRINN on IP address matters.

I hold a B.Tech in Information Technology from BIET Jhansi. I also hold wireless certifications (CWNA, CWSP, CWDP, CWISA) and I am IPv6 Sage certified by Hurricane Electric.

I write a technical blog at blog.gauravkansal.in. I write about DNS, IPv6, TLS and Wi-Fi.

Outside work, I have a deep interest in Indian history.
